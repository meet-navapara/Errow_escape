Arrow Escape

Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape works without an account. If you sign in with Google, your game progress is backed up to your private cloud record. Ads appear only if you choose to watch one for a reward.

Who we are

Arrow Escape ("the Game") is published by Meet Navapara ("we", "us"). Contact: meet56256@gmail.com.

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device: unlocked and completed levels, daily and weekly challenge results, achievements, tutorial progress, power-up balances and your sound and vibration settings.

In guest mode the Game creates a random guest identifier that stays on your device. Guest data is not sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

Sign-in is optional and uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (provided by Google LLC). We receive your Google account name, email address, profile photo link and a unique user ID. Firebase may also process technical data such as IP address and sign-in times to provide and secure sign-in.

Your game progress (the items listed above, plus record timestamps) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in a record that only your signed-in account can read or change. We use it only to back up your progress and restore it on your devices.

Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch an ad to earn an extra power-up. No ad is required to play or finish a level.

AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA where permitted), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and interaction and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and limit fraud on ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested. You can review your choice at any time in Me > Privacy Preferences.

What we do not do

The Game does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, does not sell personal information, does not offer in-app purchases and does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Service providers

Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting, Google Sign-In, AdMob and the User Messaging Platform). Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Device data stays until you delete it (Settings > Reset Progress), clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the Game at Settings > Delete Account & Cloud Data, which permanently deletes your cloud progress record and your Arrow Escape sign-in account and wipes the Game's data on that device. You can also request deletion without the app: see https://arrow-escape-85dcd.web.app/delete-account. Google may keep limited logs for security and legal purposes as described in its policies.

Security

Cloud data is transferred over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that allow only your account to access your record. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact meet56256@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Contact meet56256@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this policy if our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the Game.
