Delete your Arrow Escape account

How to delete your Arrow Escape account and the data associated with it, published by Meet Navapara.

In the app (fastest)

Open Arrow Escape, go to Me > Settings > Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm with your Google account. This immediately and permanently deletes your cloud progress record and your Arrow Escape sign-in account, and wipes the Game's data on that device.

Without the app

Email meet56256@gmail.com from the address of your Google account with the subject "Delete my Arrow Escape account". We will delete your account and its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account (name, email, user ID) and your cloud progress record: levels, daily and weekly results, achievements, tutorial progress, power-up balances, settings and timestamps.

Guest progress stored only on a device is removed by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage.

Google may retain limited security and legal logs as described in its own policies.
